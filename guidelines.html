Swachh Bharat 2035 — The Waste-Free India Challenge

School and college teams: submit your waste management solutions and compete nationally. Prototype, innovation, and impact.

About

For students and young innovators

The Swachh Bharat 2035 Challenge is an opportunity for school and college teams to design, develop and showcase waste management solutions that address real problems in their communities.

Whether it's a prototype, working model, or innovative approach—we want to see your ideas and impact.

Key Details

  • Deadline 11 October 2026
  • Team Size 2–3 students per team
  • Categories Multiple
  • Registration Fee NIL
  • Prizes Top 3 teams
Judging

How projects are evaluated

Winning projects are judged on six core criteria reflecting real-world waste management requirements.

⚙️

Functionality

Does the solution work as intended? Is it practical and implementable?

💡

Innovation

Is it a novel approach? Does it bring fresh thinking to waste management?

🎯

Practicality

Can it be realistically deployed? Are materials and methods accessible?

🌍

Environmental Impact

What are the measurable benefits? How much waste reduced or resources recovered?

💰

Affordability

Can it be implemented at reasonable cost? Is it economically viable?

📈

Scalability

Can the model be replicated in other communities or scaled up?

Submissions

What to submit

Project Information

  • Project title and description (200 words max)
  • Problem statement: What waste issue are you solving?
  • Solution approach: How does your solution work?
  • Environmental impact: What results do you expect?
  • Estimated cost to implement

Evidence

  • Photographs of your prototype or working model
  • Video demonstration (optional but recommended)
  • Test results or evidence of efficacy
  • Where has it been tested (if applicable)?

Team Details

  • School or college name and location
  • Principal or head of institution approval
  • Team member names, ages, and contact details
  • Faculty mentor (if applicable)
