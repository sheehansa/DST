Home / Symposium

A national platform for practical waste solutions

The Government of Goa, Department of Science & Technology and Waste Management is convening leading voices in waste management, circular economy, and innovation.

Purpose

Show what works.
Share what can be replicated.

The symposium aims to showcase and disseminate successful centralised and decentralised waste-management models implemented in Goa, while facilitating exchange of practical knowledge among States, Union Territories, Urban Local Bodies and other institutions.

Who should attend?

  • 🏛️ Government officials & policymakers
  • 🏢 Urban Local Bodies & municipalities
  • 🚀 Innovators, startups & entrepreneurs
  • 🎓 Academic & research institutions
  • ♻️ Industry & environmental professionals

Public Participation

🌱

Waste-to-Resource

Positioning Goa as a platform for practical, replicable solutions.
